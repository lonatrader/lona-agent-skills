---
name: lona-data
description: Find and inspect market data available through LONA.
---

Search the requested ticker or instrument with `lona_search_symbols_by_name`, or browse `lona_list_symbols`. Inspect the selected result using `lona_get_symbol`, preserving exchange, equity market, coverage, and cached frequencies. Follow the market-data skill to prepare missing supported global data with `lona_download_market_data` and its `symbol_id`. Do not assume provider coverage means the data is cached or launch a backtest just to inspect data.
